4Journey Highlights

A Journey to the Abode of Shiva

Why Mount Kailash is sacred

Mount Kailash (6,638 m) is revered by four religions — Hinduism as the abode of Lord Shiva and Parvati, Buddhism as the home of Demchok, Jainism as Ashtapad where Rishabhadeva attained liberation, and the ancient Bon faith as the axis of the world. No human has ever climbed it; pilgrims instead walk the 52 km Parikrama (Kora), believed to cleanse the sins of a lifetime.

Nearby, Lake Mansarovar (4,558 m) — the highest freshwater lake in the world — was created in the mind of Lord Brahma. A holy dip and puja on its shores, especially under the Full Moon (Purnima), is considered one of the most auspicious acts a pilgrim can perform.

  • Pashupatinath & Boudhanath darshan
  • Holy dip, puja & hawan at Mansarovar
  • First darshan of Mount Kailash

  • Dolma La Pass (5,630 m)
  • Gauri Kund — Parvati's lake
  • Zuthulpuk — Milarepa's cave
  • Purnima night at the lake

13-Day Overland Itinerary — preview

For Indian passports; NRI / foreign passports follow a 14-day version. Tap any day to expand. Timings may change with weather, roads and border rules.

Kathmandu → Kerung → Saga → Mansarovar → Darchen → Kora → Kathmandu

DAY1Arrive in Kathmandu (1,400 m)Airport pickup · 3-star hotel
Airport pickup 3-star hotel, Kathmandu Dinner

Our representative welcomes you at Tribhuvan International Airport and transfers you to the hotel. Evening yatra briefing — we collect original passports for the China group visa and hand over your Yatra kit (duffle bag, cap, jacket, trekking stick).

DAY2Kathmandu Darshan — Pashupatinath & BoudhanathUNESCO heritage sites
UNESCO heritage sites Kathmandu B · L · D

Morning darshan at Pashupatinath Temple, Boudhanath Stupa and Budhanilkantha (Sleeping Vishnu). Afternoon free for last-minute shopping. Visa processing continues.

DAY3Drive Kathmandu → Rasuwagadhi border → Kerung (2,800 m)~160 km · 7–8 hrs
~160 km · 7–8 hrs Nepal–China immigration Hotel, Kerung

Early departure along the Trishuli river and through Langtang National Park to Syabrubesi / Timure. Complete Nepal exit and Chinese immigration at Rasuwagadhi–Kerung, then a short 38 km drive to Kerung town. (If border timing requires, we overnight at Timure and cross next morning.)

DAY4Acclimatisation Day in KerungRest · Pakpa Monastery
Rest & acclimatise Pakpa Monastery Hotel, Kerung

A crucial rest day for your body to adjust to altitude before the plateau. Gentle walk to the ancient Pakpa Monastery and Kerung market. Drink plenty of water, avoid exertion.

DAY5Drive Kerung → Saga (4,450 m)232 km · Labug La pass 5,050 m
232 km · 6–7 hrs Labug La pass 5,050 m Hotel, Saga

Climb out of the Kerung valley onto the Tibetan Plateau, crossing Thong La and Labug La. Stunning views of Shishapangma and turquoise Peiku Tso lake before crossing the Brahmaputra (Yarlung Tsangpo) to reach Saga.

DAY6Drive Saga → Lake Mansarovar (4,558 m)First darshan of Kailash
~450 km · 8–9 hrs Mayum La pass Guesthouse, Mansarovar

A long but breathtaking drive across the plateau via Mayum La. Your first darshan of Mount Kailash appears on the horizon before you descend to the shores of Lake Mansarovar. Overnight in a lakeside guesthouse — on Full-Moon departures, tonight you watch Purnima rise over the holy lake.

DAY7Mansarovar Puja, Holy Bath & Hawan → Darchen (4,660 m)Holy dip & rituals
Holy dip & rituals Lake parikrama by bus + 35 km Hotel, Darchen

Sunrise holy bath in Lake Mansarovar, followed by puja and hawan on the shore. Vehicle parikrama of the lake with views of Rakshas Tal and Gurla Mandhata. Drive to Darchen, base of Mount Kailash. Arrange porters / ponies for the Kora.

DAY8Kora Day 1 — Yam Dwar → Dirapuk (4,900 m)12 km trek · Kailash north face
12 km trek · 5–6 hrs Kailash north face Basic guesthouse, Dirapuk

Short drive to Yam Dwar (Tarboche), gateway of the Parikrama. Trek gently up the Lha Chu valley with Kailash's west face beside you. Dirapuk offers the closest, most dramatic view of the sheer north face of Kailash.

DAY9Kora Day 2 — Dirapuk → Dolma La (5,630 m) → Zuthulpuk22 km trek · highest point of the yatra
22 km trek · 8–9 hrs Highest point of the yatra Basic guesthouse, Zuthulpuk

The most demanding and most rewarding day. Pre-dawn start, steady climb to Dolma La Pass with prayer flags fluttering, then descend past the emerald Gauri Kund (Parvati's lake) into the Lham Chu valley to Zuthulpuk — the cave monastery of Milarepa.

DAY10Kora Day 3 — Zuthulpuk → Darchen → Saga8 km trek · Parikrama complete
8 km trek · 2–3 hrs ~470 km drive Hotel, Saga

Easy walk out of the valley to complete the sacred Parikrama. Our vehicle waits to take you back to Darchen for lunch, then the long drive back to Saga. Celebrate — you have completed the Kailash Kora.

DAY11Drive Saga → Kerung232 km · 6–7 hrs
232 km · 6–7 hrs Hotel, Kerung B · L · D

Retrace the plateau route with a final look at Shishapangma and Peiku Tso, descending into the greener Kerung valley — warmer air and thicker oxygen at last.

DAY12Drive Kerung → Border → KathmanduFarewell dinner & certificate
Immigration at Rasuwagadhi ~160 km · 7–8 hrs Hotel, Kathmandu

Exit China, re-enter Nepal and drive back to Kathmandu. Evening farewell dinner and certificate of Yatra completion.

DAY13Departure from KathmanduAirport drop
Airport drop Breakfast

Transfer to the airport for your onward flight, or extend your trip with a Muktinath darshan, Everest mountain flight or Nepal tour. Om Namah Shivaya.

Choosing a Tour

Overland or helicopter — which should I choose?
The Overland tour from Kathmandu is our most popular — best value, gradual acclimatisation and Full-Moon dates. It takes 13 days for Indian passports and 14 days for NRI / foreign passports. The Helicopter tour (9 days, Ex. Lucknow) saves long drives by flying to Simikot and taking a helicopter to the Hilsa border. The Lhasa route adds Potala Palace and Tibetan culture. The 3-day Parikrama over Dolma La is the same on every route, so the fitness needed is similar. Try the "Help me choose" tool above.
Who can join? Do I need to be Indian or win a lottery?
No lottery and no government selection. Anyone aged 12–70 with a valid passport can join — Indian, NRI / OCI, Nepali, and all other nationalities. Pilgrims over 70 may join with a doctor's certificate and our approval. Solo travellers share a twin room with a same-gender pilgrim.

Health & Safety

How fit do I need to be? I have never trekked before.
The yatra is moderate to challenging mainly because of altitude, not technical difficulty. Most pilgrims are ordinary devotees aged 40–65. Begin daily walking and light cardio 2–3 months before and get a doctor's clearance. Ponies and porters are available on the Kora for those who need support.
Is it safe? What if I get altitude sickness?
Safety is our first priority. The itinerary ascends gradually, every vehicle carries oxygen and a first-aid kit, and guides monitor pilgrims daily. If someone cannot continue, we arrange descent by vehicle and, in emergencies, helicopter evacuation covered by your insurance.
